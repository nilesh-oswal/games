If you talk fast

  • A yellow pause mark in the script means a full two-second stop. Count it in your head.
  • If a section shows Too fast, run just that section again and slow the stories down, not the facts.
  • If you blank, think "subway." Every section ties back to the violinist and the seven who stopped.
  • Skipped a point? Keep going. Nobody in the audience knows the script.
