A little friendly rivalry.

  1. Make your teams. Use “Edit teams” for names and starting scores. One person hosts from the laptop; everyone watches the TV.
  2. Choose a clue. Pick a team, then any points tile. Each board has 6 categories and 30 questions, from 100 to 500 points.
  3. You have 90 seconds. The timer starts when a clue opens. Pause or restart it whenever needed. At zero, it stops; the answer stays hidden until the host reveals it.
  4. Reveal and award. Choose the answering team, reveal the answer, then award points. If nobody gets it, choose “No winner.” Accept equivalent wording and Hindi or English answers; no question-form phrasing required.
  5. Keep the party moving. Switch between all four boards whenever you like. Scores carry across them. “Undo” reverses the last scoring, team edit, or restart action.

Before reveal, closing a clue saves it for later. After reveal, closing uses the clue without awarding points. “New night” clears all boards and scores but keeps team names.

Everything runs offline. Scores and played clues save in this browser when browser storage is available. An unfinished question reopens fresh after a page reload.

Start a new game night?

This clears all 120 played clues and sets every team’s score to zero. Your team names stay. You can undo this until you reload the page.
