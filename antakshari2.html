How to host / खेल कैसे चलाएँ 🎤

Use the Language / भाषा selector to show questions and answers in English, Hindi, or both. / भाषा विकल्प से सवाल और जवाब हिंदी, अंग्रेज़ी या दोनों में देखें।

  1. Set team names. Teams alternate through 50 curated songs.
  2. Read the clue aloud, or show it on a big screen.
  3. Start the 45-second timer once the team is ready.
  4. They must name the specific song and sing at least two recognizable lines.
  5. Give 15 points for naming and singing; 5 points for naming only; 0 points for pass or a wrong answer.
  6. Tap “Reveal answer” to check the title, movie, singers, and music director. Answer reveals pause the timer.
  7. The lyrics lookup opens a web search; the optional full-lyrics field stores text you supply on your device.
  8. Undo replays the previous question. “New game” shuffles question order and resets scores without deleting your added lyrics.

Song credits have been curated for a fun party game; hosts should review any disputed credits or alternate versions before the event.
